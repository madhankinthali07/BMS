How to Generate App Password

Takes less than 60 seconds.

Google requires an App Password for safe background notification automation:

  1. Visit your Google Account Security Settings →
  2. Ensure 2-Step Verification is enabled on your Gmail.
  3. Go directly to Google App Passwords →
  4. Under "App name", enter Showzy and click Create.
  5. Copy the generated 16-character code (e.g. abcd efgh ijkl mnop).
  6. Paste it in the left form and click "Save & Connect Gmail".
